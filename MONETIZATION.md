# Midnight Walk — Monetization Plan

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Template Marketplace
- **Free**: 3 base templates
- **Pro ($9/mo)**: 15+ templates, AI generation, unlimited exports
- **Agency ($29/mo)**: Team access, custom scenes, priority support

### 2. Asset Store
- **Free**: 20 PNG cutouts, 4 generated WebP cards
- **Pro ($9/mo)**: Full library, commercial license

### 3. Hosting
- **Free**: GitHub Pages
- **Pro ($9/mo)**: Custom domain, SSL, CDN, analytics
- **Agency ($29/mo)**: White-label, custom domains, SLA

### 4. Consulting
- Custom scene design: $500-2000 per scene
- Brand integration: $1000-5000
- Performance audit: $200-500
